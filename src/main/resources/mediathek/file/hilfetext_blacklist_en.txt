The blacklist hides films that match a row in the table.

The whitelist shows only films that match a row in the table.

Switching between blacklist and whitelist reverses which films are displayed.

Channel and topic must match exactly. The title filter must occur within the film title. The topic/title filter must occur in either the film topic or its title.

Matching is case-insensitive.

The Title and Topic/title fields also accept regular expressions, introduced by "#:". Regular expressions are also case-insensitive.

#:Abend.*

This matches films whose corresponding field begins with "Abend" or "abend".
